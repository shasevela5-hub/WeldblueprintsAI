Is this really a one-time payment?

Yes. Pay once and keep Pro access permanently. There are no monthly fees or renewals in this flow.

What happens to the free limit?

Free accounts keep the 3-generation cap. After upgrading, blueprint generation becomes unlimited.

Do I need an account before upgrading?

Yes. The Pro status is attached to your signed-in account, so create or log into an account first.

What if payment succeeds but activation fails?

The page will show an error and you should keep the PayPal order ID. That is enough to resolve the upgrade on the server side.
